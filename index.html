Privacy Policy

Aquarium Tracker — Last updated: November 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Aquarium Tracker ("the app", "we", "us") handles information. The app is provided by the developer identified at the contact address below. By using the app, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Overview

Aquarium Tracker is a water intake tracker. It lets you log drinks, track daily hydration for yourself and a pet, view statistics, set a daily reminder, and use a home screen widget. The app is designed to work entirely on your device.

We do not operate servers for the app, we do not have user accounts, and we do not collect, transmit, or share any personal information with anyone.

2. Data stored on your device

The app stores the following information locally on your device only, using Android's on-device storage (DataStore). This data never leaves your device and is never sent to us or to any third party:

  • Water intake logs, cup history, and statistics
  • Your profile settings (age, weight, height, activity level, wake-up time, etc.)
  • Pet profile information (species, breed, weight, age, etc.)
  • App preferences (theme, language, aquarium mode, drink selection)
  • Achievement progress
  • Premium purchase token and entitlement status (see Section 3)

You can delete all of this data at any time by clearing the app's data in Android settings or by uninstalling the app.

3. Purchases and Google Play Billing

The app offers a one-time in-app purchase ("Premium") through Google Play Billing. When you make a purchase:

  • The payment is processed entirely by Google Play. We never see or store your payment card details, billing address, or Google account information.
  • The app receives only the purchase token and entitlement status needed to unlock Premium features on your device.
  • Purchases, refunds, and subscription management are governed by Google Play's Terms of Service and Google's Privacy Policy.

We do not use purchase information for advertising, analytics, or any other purpose.

4. Third-party services

The app uses the following third-party components, all provided by Google:

  • Google Play Billing — processes in-app purchases (see Section 3).
  • Android OS services — notifications, alarms, and app widgets are handled locally by the operating system.

The app does not contain advertising, analytics SDKs, crash reporting services, or any other third-party code that collects or transmits user data.

5. Backups

If you have Android Auto Backup enabled, the app's local data may be included in the automatic backup of your device to your own Google account. This backup is encrypted and controlled by you and Google; we do not initiate, access, or manage it. You can exclude app data from backups in your device settings.

6. Notifications

The optional daily water reminder is scheduled and delivered entirely on your device. No notification data leaves your device, and we do not send push notifications.

7. Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children. All data stays on the device.

8. Data retention and deletion

Because all data is stored locally on your device, it is retained only for as long as the app is installed. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes all locally stored information. Data included in your personal Android backups is deleted according to Google's backup retention policies.

9. Security

All app data is stored using Android's standard on-device storage mechanisms and is not transmitted over the network by the app. As the data never leaves your device, there is no transmission to intercept.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
