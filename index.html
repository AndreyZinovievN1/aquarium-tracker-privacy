Privacy Policy

Aquarium Tracker — Last updated: November 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Aquarium Tracker ("the app", "we", "us") handles information. The app is provided by the developer identified at the contact address below. By using the app, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Overview

Aquarium Tracker is a water intake tracker. It lets you log drinks, track daily hydration for yourself and a pet, view statistics, set a daily reminder, use a home screen widget, and — optionally — keep a cloud backup of your data in your own Google Drive.

The app is designed to work on your device. We do not operate servers for the app, we do not have user accounts of our own, and we do not collect personal information for our own purposes. The only network features are optional and user-initiated: an in-app purchase handled by Google Play, and a backup sync to your own Google Drive that runs only if you choose to sign in with your Google account.

2. Data stored on your device

The app stores the following information locally on your device, using Android's on-device storage (DataStore). Unless you enable the optional Drive sync described in Section 4, this data never leaves your device and is never sent to us or to any third party:

  • Water intake logs, cup history, and statistics
  • Your profile settings (age, weight, height, activity level, wake-up time, etc.)
  • Pet profile information (species, breed, weight, age, etc.)
  • App preferences (theme, language, aquarium mode, drink selection)
  • Achievement progress
  • Premium purchase token and entitlement status (see Section 3)

You can delete all of this data at any time by clearing the app's data in Android settings or by uninstalling the app.

3. Purchases and Google Play Billing

The app offers a one-time in-app purchase ("Premium") through Google Play Billing. When you make a purchase:

  • The payment is processed entirely by Google Play. We never see or store your payment card details, billing address, or Google account information.
  • The app receives only the purchase token and entitlement status needed to unlock Premium features on your device. Premium entitlement is verified through Google Play; it is not transferable through backup files.
  • Purchases and refunds are governed by Google Play's Terms of Service and Google's Privacy Policy.

We do not use purchase information for advertising, analytics, or any other purpose.

4. Optional Google account sign-in and Drive backup

The app offers an optional feature that keeps a backup of your data in the hidden per-app folder of your own Google Drive (appDataFolder). It is entirely off until you choose to use it.

If you sign in with your Google account, the app requests only:

  • your basic Google profile, including your email address, which is shown to you inside the app's backup settings; and
  • the drive.appdata OAuth scope — access to the app's own hidden Drive folder only. This scope does not grant access to any of your other Drive files, and the app never lists, reads, or modifies anything outside that folder.

When sync is enabled, the app uploads a single backup document (aquarium-backup.json) containing the same data listed in Section 2, or downloads a newer copy from your Drive if one exists. Sync runs when you open the app or tap "Sync now". The document is stored in your Drive account and is readable only by this app signed in as you. We operate no server that could read it, and the file is invisible in your regular Drive storage.

How we use Google user data. Google account information and Drive data accessed through these scopes are used only to provide the backup feature you initiated:

  • We do not use Google user data for advertising, analytics, profiling, or any purpose other than operating the backup sync for you.
  • We do not sell Google user data, and we do not transfer it to third parties except as strictly necessary to provide the feature — that is, to Google's own infrastructure (Google Sign-In, Google Drive API) acting on your behalf.
  • No human reads your backup data. Access tokens are obtained at runtime and are managed by Google Play services; the app does not store your Google password.
  • You can revoke the app's access at any time from your Google Account permissions page, sign out inside the app, or delete the cloud copy from the app's settings.

5. Other backups

If you have Android Auto Backup enabled, the app's local data may be included in the automatic backup of your device to your own Google account. This backup is encrypted and controlled by you and Google; we do not initiate, access, or manage it. You can exclude app data from backups in your device settings.

The app can also create a backup file that you save yourself — for example into cloud storage or onto another device. The file is written on your device and is only transmitted if you choose to save or share it. Restoring from it replaces the app's local data with the file's contents.

6. Notifications

The optional daily water reminder is scheduled and delivered entirely on your device. No notification data leaves your device, and we do not send push notifications.

7. Third-party services and advertising

The app uses the following third-party components, all provided by Google:

  • Google Play Billing — processes in-app purchases (see Section 3).
  • Google Sign-In and Google Drive API — optional backup sync to your own Drive's hidden app folder (see Section 4).
  • Android OS services — notifications, alarms, and app widgets are handled locally by the operating system.

The app does not contain advertising, analytics SDKs, crash reporting services, or any other third-party code that collects or transmits user data. We do not show ads and we do not track you.

8. Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children. All app data stays on the device unless the optional Drive sync described in Section 4 is enabled by an adult account holder.

9. Data retention and deletion

Local app data is retained only for as long as the app is installed. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes all locally stored information.

If you used the optional Google Drive sync, the backup copy in your Drive is retained until you choose "Delete cloud copy" in the app's settings or remove it from your Google account. Signing out of the app does not delete that file. Revoking the app's access from your Google Account permissions page stops all future syncs; you can then delete the file from your Drive manually.

Data included in your personal Android backups is deleted according to Google's backup retention policies.

10. Security

All app data is stored using Android's standard on-device storage mechanisms. Network traffic is limited to the optional Google Drive backup sync described in Section 4, which goes directly from your device to Google's servers over HTTPS under your own account. We operate no servers and therefore hold no copies of your data.
